# Board meeting, September 2026

Minutes of the board of Larkspur & Vane, held in the long room above the
office. Present: the chair, the managing director, the finance director and
one non-executive director; one director sent apologies. Fictional minutes,
written for a lab; every name, number and plan in them is invented.

## 1. The new bonus scheme

The managing director presented a profit share for every colleague who has
worked at least a year. Five percent of the annual profit above 300,000
crowns would be shared equally among eligible colleagues in March. Based on
last year's figures that would have been about 640 crowns each. The board
approved the scheme for a trial of two years. It will be announced at the
Christmas gathering.

## 2. Furnace quotes

Two quotes for relining the main furnace arrived. Holm & Daughters quoted
172,000 crowns with three weeks of work; Brightwater Kilns quoted 158,000
crowns with four weeks. The board preferred Holm & Daughters because the
fourth week would run into the February orders. The finance director will
spread the cost over two years.

## 3. Staffing

Two glassblowers will retire next summer. The board agreed to take on two
apprentices this autumn rather than hire experienced makers, so that the
apprentices can work beside the retiring makers for a full year.

## 4. Any other business

The chair asked that the bonus scheme stay inside the board collection until
the Christmas gathering, so that colleagues hear it from the managing
director and not from a rumour.

Next meeting: October 2026.
