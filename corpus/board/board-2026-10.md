# Board meeting, October 2026

Minutes of the board of Larkspur & Vane, held in the long room above the
office. Present: the full board. Fictional minutes, written for a lab; every
name, number and plan in them is invented.

## 1. Project Lighthouse

The managing director presented **Project Lighthouse**: the planned
acquisition of Saltmere Glassworks, a family glass maker forty kilometres
up the coast with eighteen people and its own sand supply. Saltmere blows the
clear lantern glass we now buy from three suppliers, and its owner wants to
retire without closing the works.

The proposed price is 1.1 million crowns, paid half on signing and half over
three years from Saltmere's own profits. Buying Saltmere would cut the cost
of a harbour lantern glass by about a third and end the delays of last
winter, when a supplier missed two deliveries.

The board approved exclusive talks until 31 January 2027. The finance
director will lead the due diligence with an outside accountant. Nobody
outside the board may know the name of the glassworks until both sides have
signed; inside the company the deal is called Lighthouse and nothing else.

## 2. Risks

- Saltmere's furnace is older than ours and may need relining within a year.
- Two of Saltmere's glassblowers are close to retirement.
- If the talks leak, a larger competitor may make a higher offer.

The board asked the managing director to keep a written list of everyone
who knows the name, and to read it out at every board meeting until the
talks end.

## 3. Furnace

The relining by Holm & Daughters is booked for the last three weeks of
January. The hot shop will build stock in December to cover the stop.

Next meeting: November 2026.
