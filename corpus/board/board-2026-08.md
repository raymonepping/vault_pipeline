# Board meeting, August 2026

Minutes of the board of Larkspur & Vane, held in the long room above the
office. Present: the chair, the managing director, the finance director and
two non-executive directors. Fictional minutes, written for a lab; every
name, number and plan in them is invented.

## 1. Results for the first half year

Revenue for January to June came to 2.4 million crowns, eight percent above
last year. The harbour lantern range sold out twice. Margins fell from 31 to
28 percent because the price of tin rose by a fifth since March. The finance
director expects the rise to hold until at least the spring.

## 2. Pricing

The board discussed the price list for the autumn catalogue. The managing
director proposed raising the harbour range by 6 percent and the garden range
by 4 percent from 1 October, and keeping the children's night lantern at its
current price, because it brings families into the shops. The board agreed.

The engraved lanterns will move from a fixed price to a price per letter: 3
crowns a letter with a minimum of 40 crowns. The sales team will tell the
three largest shop customers in person before the catalogue goes out.

## 3. The furnace

The main furnace is nineteen years old. Its refractory lining needs replacing
within two years. A full relining costs about 180,000 crowns and stops the
hot shop for three weeks. The board asked for two quotes before the October
meeting and asked the hot shop lead to plan the stop for the quietest weeks
of the year, in late January.

## 4. Any other business

The chair reminded the directors that board papers stay in the board
collection and are not discussed on the workshop floor until the managing
director announces a decision.

Next meeting: September 2026.
