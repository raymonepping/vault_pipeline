# Safety at the forge

The hot shop is the heart of Larkspur & Vane and the one place where a small
mistake hurts. Read this page before your first shift and again after your
first month. Everything in it is fictional and written for a lab, but the
habits are real ones.

## Before you enter

- Sign the forge safety briefing with your mentor. Without it, the key card
  does not open the hot shop door.
- Wear the leather apron, the green-tinted glasses and closed shoes with a
  leather upper. Synthetic clothes melt; wear cotton or wool.
- Tie back long hair and take off rings and bracelets. Glass at working heat
  is about 1100 degrees and it forgives nothing.

## The furnace

Only the hot shop lead and the two senior glassblowers may open the furnace
door or change its settings. Everyone else gathers glass from the glory hole,
the small opening on the left, using a preheated pipe from the rack. A cold
pipe in hot glass cracks and sprays.

The furnace runs day and night from Monday morning to Friday afternoon. At
the weekend it idles at 900 degrees. If the furnace alarm sounds, step back
to the yellow line, do not touch the door, and wait for the hot shop lead.

## Burns

Cool a burn under running water for twenty minutes, even if it stops hurting
after two. The first aid kit hangs next to the sink by the annealer, and the
first aiders this year are Joost and Femke. Every burn, even a small one, goes
into the red book on the shop lead's desk. We read the red book every month
and change something when a burn happens twice in the same place.

## The annealer

Finished glass rests in the annealer for eight hours so it cools without
cracking. Never open the annealer before the green light shows. A lantern
glass taken out early may look perfect and still burst on the customer's
table a week later. That is the worst kind of mistake: the kind that looks
like success.
