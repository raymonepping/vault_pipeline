# Travel and expenses

Most of us never travel for work. The sales team visits shops and fairs, the
engravers sometimes visit a customer to measure a doorway, and twice a year a
small group goes to the glass fair in Tollerby. This page covers what the
company pays for and how to claim it. Like everything in this handbook, it is
fictional and written for a lab.

## Before you go

Ask your bench lead or the office for approval at least five working days
before the trip. Fairs are booked by the office, including the stand, the
train tickets and the guest house. For a customer visit, book the train
yourself in second class; a car from the pool is fine when you carry samples
heavier than a full lantern crate.

## What we pay for

- Second class train tickets, or the pool car and its fuel.
- A guest house or a simple hotel, up to 120 crowns a night.
- Meals on the road, up to 45 crowns a day, with receipts.
- A taxi after nine in the evening, or when you carry glass.

We do not pay for first class tickets, minibar items, or a hotel upgrade.
If a customer invites you to dinner, let them; if you invite a customer,
ask the office first.

## Filing a claim

File every travel expense within **30 days** of the day you come back.
Use the blue expense form in the office or the form on the workshop
terminal, attach every receipt, and give it to Marta. Claims that arrive
after 30 days are paid only with your bench lead's written approval, and the
office will remind you of that with a sigh.

The office pays approved claims with the next monthly salary. If a single
claim is more than 400 crowns, the office can pay it within a week; say so on
the form.

## Lost receipts

It happens. Write down what you paid, where and when, sign it, and attach it
to the claim with a note. One lost receipt a quarter is forgiven without
questions. Two is a conversation with your bench lead about pockets.
